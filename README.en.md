PS Affiliate lets visitors recommend your website. It attributes visits to partners and displays recorded results in the user area and WordPress dashboard.

> **For website owners and partners:** This guide covers setup and everyday use—no developer documentation.

## What PS Affiliate does

- Creates personal referral links for partners.
- Reports visits, registrations, and recorded results.
- Provides tools to manage partners and account balances.
- Can optionally make banners available to partners.
- Can connect to PS Bloghosting, MarketPress, and PS Memberships through compatible add-ons.

Which registrations or purchases are tracked and rewarded depends on the active add-ons and their settings. Without a compatible add-on, the plugin primarily tracks referral visits.

## Setup for website owners

1. Install and activate PS Affiliate in the WordPress dashboard.
2. Go to **Affiliates → Settings** and review the partner-program settings.
3. Decide whether partners need approval before payment and whether you want to offer banners.
4. If needed, activate the compatible add-on for your shop, membership, or hosting plugin.
5. Enable the partner program for users, or ask them to activate their own account under **Users → Affiliate Referrals**.

You can view reports and manage partners under **Affiliates**.

## Getting started as a partner

1. Sign in to the website.
2. Go to **Users → Affiliate Referrals**.
3. Enable your partner account and add your PayPal address if needed.
4. Copy your personal referral link and share it.
5. View your report on the same page. If the website offers banners, you can find them there too.

Referrals are normally attributed to a partner for up to **30 days**. Attribution uses a browser cookie, so browser settings or deleted cookies may prevent it.

## Accounts and payments

PS Affiliate records and manages reports and account balances, but it does **not** make automatic payments. Website owners review the amounts and arrange payments themselves. A payment file can be prepared for PayPal Mass Pay.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are purchases tracked automatically?

Only when a compatible add-on for your shop, membership, or hosting plugin is active and configured. The add-on and partner-program settings determine which activities can earn a commission.

### Why don't I see any results yet?

Check that your partner account is enabled, that you are using your personal referral link, and that the required add-on for registrations or purchases is set up. Visits and results will appear after someone follows your link.

### What should I know about privacy?

PS Affiliate uses a browser cookie to attribute referrals. Check the privacy and cookie requirements that apply to your website and update your privacy notice if needed.

## Requirements

PS Affiliate requires WordPress 4.9 or later and PHP 8.1 or later. It can also be used on a WordPress Multisite installation.
